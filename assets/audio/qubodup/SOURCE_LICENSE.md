Included files (original OGG bytes, no re-encoding):

- `wardrobe_close.ogg`: `ogg/qubodup-DoorClose02.ogg`.
- `wardrobe_open.ogg`: `ogg/qubodup-DoorOpen01.ogg`.

The downloaded archive and full set are retained under `tools/audio_sources/`, excluded from Godot import by `tools/.gdignore`. Only these two selected recordings are active game assets. These are recordings of doors used for the wardrobe interaction, not recordings of this game's fictional wardrobe.
